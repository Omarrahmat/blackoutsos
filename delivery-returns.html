Delivery and returns

Three working days, free, tracked.

Every bag is assembled to order in the UK and dispatched within three working days. UK delivery is free and tracked: Royal Mail Tracked 48 for the backpacks, a courier for the Unit. You get the tracking number by email when it leaves.

Delivery

Free, tracked, to any UK address including Northern Ireland, the Highlands and the islands. Signed for on delivery for the Unit. International delivery is coming with our US and European launches; until then, email us and we'll quote.

What's inside, and the post

Bags contain lithium batteries and a power bank, which travel by ground under the carrier's rules, and no matches or lighters, which can't be posted at all. The multi-tool is UK-carry-legal: non-locking, blade under 7.62 cm.

Returns

Thirty days to return an unopened bag for a full refund: seals intact, contents unused. Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations you can cancel within 14 days of receiving your order for any reason; sealed hygiene and food items that have been unsealed can't be returned. Faulty items are replaced first, refunded second.

How to return something

Email hello@blackoutsos.com with your order number and what's wrong. We send a prepaid label the same working day for faults, and a returns address for changes of mind. Refunds reach your card within five working days of the bag reaching us.
