What Ireland plans for

The main hazards.

  • Storms and high winds
  • Coastal and river flooding
  • Snow and ice
  • Power outages

Your 72 hours

Three days of water (about 3 litres per person per day for drinking, more for washing), food that needs no cooking, light, warmth, first aid, a week of medicines, copies of documents, some cash and a plan for the family and pets.
