Official numbers

Who to call. Write these on the card.

These are the UK numbers that matter in the first hour. They are printed on the contacts card in every bag.

999Emergency: life at risk, fire, crime in progress
105Power cut: free, any network, connects to your local operator
111NHS: urgent but not life-threatening
0345 988 1188Floodline: warnings and advice, 24 hours
0800 111 999Gas emergency: smell gas, open windows, get out, call
