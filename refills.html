Refills and rotation

Your bag tells you when it's time.

Every bag carries a rotation card and a QR code. Scan it once, tell us which bag and when you bought it, and we'll remind you when the water, the rations or the batteries are due and when the power bank wants charging. When something is due, a refill pack arrives with exactly what to swap and nothing else.
