The 72-hour kit, US version

What the official checklist asks for.

From Ready.gov's basic disaster supplies kit, with what the Standard Issue already carries. In an emergency call 911; for official alerts keep Wireless Emergency Alerts switched on and follow your state and county emergency management.

WaterOne gallon per person per day, for at least three days, for drinking and sanitation (Ready.gov). The Standard Issue carries water pouches and purification tablets; store the gallons at home.
FoodThree days of food that needs no cooking or fridge. The Standard Issue carries one 500 g emergency food ration (2,500 kcal) with a five-year shelf life.
Light and powerFlashlight, spare batteries, a power bank, a hand-crank or battery NOAA Weather Radio at home.
First aid and medicinesA first-aid kit, and a week of prescription medicines with a copy of each prescription.
DocumentsCopies of IDs, insurance policies and bank records in a waterproof sleeve, plus some cash in small bills.
Warmth and shelterEmergency blankets, a rain poncho, warm layers; in hurricane country, work gloves and a whistle.
Family and petsA meeting place, an out-of-state contact everyone knows, and a kit for each child and pet.
