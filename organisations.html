For organisations

Every person you're responsible for, ready for three days.

Power cuts, storms, floods, heat and network outages don't wait for office hours. Organisations around the world use Blackout SOS to look after their people: one bag per person, packed for who's carrying it, kept in date by us.

Who we work with

Offices and workplaces

A bag at every desk or on every floor, written into your business-continuity and duty-of-care plans. When the building loses power, nobody has to improvise.

Schools and universities

Grab bags for classrooms and halls of residence, a Unit for every evacuation point, and refills scheduled around the academic year.

Hotels and hospitality

A discreet bag per room or per floor and a team bag at reception, so guests are looked after when the lights go out.

Property and residential

Bags for every apartment, concierge and building manager, with a rotation schedule we run so the kit is live when the building isn't.

Care, health and support providers

Bags for residents, clients and the staff who look after them, packed for the people you support, with the emergency-plan wording written for you.

Charities and NGOs

Volume pricing for the communities you serve, co-branded warning alerts, and one bag donated for every twenty sold.

Animal rescue and rehoming

A Scout with every adoption, so the dog or cat goes home with its own bag.

Corporate gifting

A gift that says you care whether your people get home safely. Custom name cards and a note from you in every bag.

How it works

Tell us who the bags are for.

  1. Tell us how many people, where they are, and who they are: staff, guests, residents, students.
  2. We send a quote in your currency, a sample bag if you'd like one, and the wording for your plan.
  3. Bags arrive assembled and labelled, in one delivery or staggered across sites.
  4. We keep the dates and send refills before anything expires, invoiced the same way.
