For organisations

One bag per placement, per desk, per home.

Children's homes, fostering agencies, councils, charities, rehoming centres and workplaces buy in tens and hundreds. Volume pricing from ten bags, invoice terms for public bodies and charities, the emergency-policy wording written for you, and a rotation schedule we manage so the bags are live when they're needed.

Who we work with

Children's homes

A Recruit for every child and a Unit for every home, written into the home's emergency policy and the welcome pack. We supply the policy wording.

Fostering agencies

A Recruit per placement at agency pricing, with the carer guides. A line in the placement checklist, not another task.

Councils and resilience forums

Operators for vulnerable residents in flood-risk wards, grab bags for wardens and rest-centre teams. Pilots from 50.

Charities and foodbanks

A give-back scheme: one bag donated for every twenty sold, and co-branded Be Ready alerts for the people you support.

Animal rehoming

A Scout with every adoption, with a co-branded pet card. The dog or cat leaves with its own bag.

Workplaces and housing

A bag per desk or per flat, a rotation schedule we manage, and a yearly check-in so the kit is live when the building isn't.

How it works

Ask, and we'll write the policy with you.

  1. Tell us who the bags are for and how many.
  2. We send a quote, a sample bag if you want one, and the policy wording for your emergency plan.
  3. Bags arrive assembled, with write-on name cards, in one delivery or staggered.
  4. We hold the rotation dates and send the refills when they're due, invoiced the same way.

Received. A person will reply within two working days.
