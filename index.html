Built for the dark, not for the shelf.

Most kits are a rucksack with things thrown in. Ours is a system: every item has a pouch, every pouch has a place, and the bag itself is built to be found, opened and emptied with one hand in the rain.

01 · Waterproof

TPU-laminated Cordura, taped seams, AquaGuard zips

The shell is the same waterproof laminate used on expedition packs. Ten minutes under a shower head, nothing inside gets wet.

02 · The Morse mark

Three short, three long, three short

SOS, laser-cut into the side panel and raised on the patch. Anyone who knows it, knows. Everyone else sees a clean black bag.

03 · Found in the dark

Name window, colour tag, reflective piping

Your name and medical notes by the handle. A tag colour per person. Piping that reads black by day and lights up in a torch beam.

04 · Honest food

15-year rations, vegan, made without nuts

Every bag is vegan and gluten-free on the food. The only exception is the Cub's infant formula, and we say so on the box.
