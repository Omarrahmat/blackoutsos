Questions

Straight answers.

Is the multi-tool legal to carry in the UK?

Yes. The blade is non-locking and under 7.62 cm, which is the legal limit for carrying without a specific reason. No locking knives, ever.

Why is there no lighter or matches?

They can't be posted. The ferro rod lights a stove or tinder for years and travels anywhere.

Is three days of water really in there?

No, and nobody's is. Three days of water weighs nine kilos. The bag carries the first day and the tablets and bottle to treat fifty litres more. Store water at home; carry the means to make it safe.

Can I carry it on a plane?

The bag fits cabin size. The multi-tool and ferro rod would have to go in the hold; the power bank must stay in the cabin. We include a card that lists it.

What if I already have some of this?

Twenty litres are left empty for a reason. Add what you have; the pouches are labelled so you'll still find everything in the dark.
