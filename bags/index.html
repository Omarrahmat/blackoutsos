Standard build

Every bag, every time

500D TPU-laminated Cordura, taped seams, YKK AquaGuard zips, a 1000D base. Name window by the handle with a write-on card and marker. Side bottle pocket. Whistle on a lanyard and one in the buckle. Reflective piping. The Morse SOS cut into the side.

Honest food

Vegan, made without nut ingredients

NRG-5 Zero rations: 2,325 kcal per 500 g, labelled vegan and gluten-free, 15-year date. No sweets in a child's bag. The Cub's infant formula is the one exception in the range and the box says so.

Nothing bespoke

Ships the day you order

Names go on the card with the marker in the lid, by you. Nothing is printed to order, so every bag is a standard product, in stock, assembled in the UK and dispatched within three working days.
