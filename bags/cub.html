Questions

Straight answers.

Why is there only one bottle?

Because sterile teats and cold-water tablets do the job of five bottles in a tenth of the space. A fresh sealed teat for every feed, and the one bottle sterilised in a bag of water in fifteen minutes, no power needed.

Why ready-to-feed and not powder?

Powdered formula is not sterile and needs water at 70 degrees or hotter to make it safe. In a power cut you may not have that. Ready-to-feed cartons are sterile until opened. The NHS says the same.

I breastfeed. Do I need the Cub?

Yes, for everything except the formula: nappies, warmth, wipes, the carrier, the mat, the first aid, and two pouches of water for you. Keep the formula sealed as the backup every parent hopes not to use.

Is the Cub vegan?

Everything except the formula, which is dairy-based. The baby food pouches are fruit and vegetable recipes made without nut ingredients. The formula is the only exception in the range and the box says so.

What about twins?

Two Cubs, one per baby, as with every bag in the range. Both clip onto one pram.

What happens when my baby grows?

Scan the rotation card and we remind you. The stage refill swaps the nappies, the feeding and the tag dots; the bag stays.
