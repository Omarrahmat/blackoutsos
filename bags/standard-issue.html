Questions

Straight answers.

Is the bag waterproof?

Yes. The rucksack itself is waterproof, so rain stays out and the kit inside stays dry. It isn't made to be submerged, so don't wade with it under water.

Who is it for?

One adult for 72 hours. Buy one per adult in the house. Bags for children, babies, families and pets are coming in 2027.

Is there a range of bags?

Not yet. The Standard Issue is our only bag for now: a plain rucksack packed with a complete 72-hour kit. Our own purpose-built range is planned for 2027; join the list on the homepage to hear first.

How long does it last?

The water and the food ration keep for five years. Sign up when you order and we email you when anything is due to be replaced.

Is there enough food?

Yes, for survival. The Seven Oceans 500 g ration gives 2,500 kcal in 9 individually wrapped bars and is made to the international standard for one person's emergency food (10,000 kJ). It's survival food, not full meals, so keep normal food at home too.

Is there enough water?

No bag can carry three days of water for drinking and washing: that's about 9 litres. The Standard Issue carries 1.5 litres plus a bottle and purification tablets so you can make more safe water as you go.

When will it arrive?

Packed and dispatched within three working days. UK delivery is free and tracked.
