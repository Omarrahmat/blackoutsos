Questions

Straight answers.

Why is the food canister empty?

Because a sudden change of food upsets a pet's stomach, and the right food is the one they eat today. Fill the canister from the bag you already buy and swap it every three months.

Will the rest centre take my pet?

Not always. Most emergency shelters try, but it is not guaranteed, so the plan is a friend or family member who will take you both, and a carrier by the door. The 'Pets in an emergency' guide walks you through it.

Why a muzzle in the dog kit?

A hurt or frightened dog can bite the person helping it. Vets and rescue teams ask for a soft muzzle; having one means your dog gets treated faster.

What about the microchip?

Keep the details up to date today; it is the law for dogs and, since 2024, for cats in England. The pet card carries the number, so anyone who finds your pet can call it in.

Two pets?

One Scout each. An Operator carries one docked bag at a time, so with two pets, two adults each take one, or the second Scout goes on its own straps.
